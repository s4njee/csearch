## Analysis (Phase 4 verdict: PASS)

- Mean overlap@10 **92.5%** (threshold ≥ 90%); 10/12 queries at 100%.
- The two sub-100% queries diverge because of **candidate-pool asymmetry in
  the harness**, not corpus deficiency: blue's top-800 ANN candidates span 27
  congresses (~11% are congress 119) while green's are all-119, so green
  surfaces deeper 119 matches blue's pool never reaches. Every green-only
  bill exists on blue with chunks — blue just didn't rank them into its pool.
- **The cap-200 fear is unsupported**: the divergent set *includes* the
  at-cap mega-bills (hr4418-119, hr5658-119 at exactly 200 chunks) being
  retrieved successfully on green and missed on blue. No re-convergence with
  a higher cap is warranted on this evidence.
- Latency: green median **16ms** vs blue **96ms** (smaller corpus + idler
  host; both well within budget).
- First run of this harness (before the congress filter) showed 6.7% overlap
  — kept as a methodology note: parity across corpora of different scope is
  meaningless without slicing to the shared corpus.
